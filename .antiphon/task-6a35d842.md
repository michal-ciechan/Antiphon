Twenty percent stays above the worst wall pair. Five percent CPU stays above that worst CPU pair and above the idle CPU median of about 0.002, and it rejects about 500 ms of extra CPU on a 10 s arm. Eight phases at 50 ms add about 400 ms of wall and almost no CPU, so the wall median is what rejects the sleep. CPU time alone would miss that sleep.
