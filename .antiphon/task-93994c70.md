**Mechanism, from the instrumented runs:**
- In the failing run, the denied child wrote its result with an in-process time of **0.026 s**, well under its 0.2 s limit. It was killed anyway at childSeconds=5.001, and the fifo child at 5.025.
- An isolated probe of the denied child (n=20 at the tip) gave wall p50 2.9–4.6 s, p95 3.4–6.2 s, max 6.19 s.
  - Dot-sourcing nightly-common plus test-phase-evidence took 2.7–5.1 s.
  - The reader itself took at most 0.032 s.
- A paired run against the base library (192f15240, n=10) gave p50 2.80 s and p95 3.64 s, against the tip's 2.94 s and 3.45 s.
- The card grows the library from 3,162 to 4,445 lines (+41%), which adds dot-source time to every child.
- The 5000 ms limits and the `:1357` bound are inherited: they come from 1a5b7c59c and e08c28e46, both ancestors of the base. The functions under test (`Read-TestPhaseJsonlBounded`, `Get-TestPhasePathIdentity`, `Read-TestPhaseBoundedBytes`, `Read-QualificationSidecar`) are byte-identical to base.
- The foreign-journal bound measured 38–348 ms. Once, the production 200 ms deadline returned the fail-closed `why=timing-read-refused`. That output is diagnostic only.

**Verdict:**
- This is not an unbounded code path.
- It is not a cold start that happens only on the first run: every child is a cold pwsh, and failures repeat as long as the host is loaded.
- It is a deadline set at roughly 1x the loaded p95, against the rule of about 2x p95.

**Fix (test only):**
- At `:534` and `:565`, raise the limit to 15000 ms (2x the observed max of 6.2 s, rounded up).
- Split each conflated `Assert-Check` into separate exited / exit / in-process-seconds checks, so a failure names the term. Keep the in-process 0.2 s checks.
- At `:1357`, assert the structural bound (40 files listed and the bytes read) and use a wall bound of at least 0.5 s, about 2x the loaded p95. Leave the production 200 ms deadline alone.
