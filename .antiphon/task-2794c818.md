### D-1 Case-folding state table drops an Ordinal-distinct obligation from L

- Where: `scripts/lib/rc-dirty-order.ps1:80` (`$states = @{}`), and its uses at `:97`, `:132`, `:141`.
- Failure: the duplicate check `$seen` is `HashSet[string](Ordinal)`, so `Suite/FooTests` and
  `Suite/fooTests` both pass. `$states` is a PowerShell `@{}` (case-insensitive), so the second
  overwrites the first. Probe at the tip: two Dirty obligations in, `launch count=1 keys=Suite/fooTests`.
  With `A` Dirty and failed plus `a` Sample, the output is only `a:failed:Sample`; `A` is gone and
  its failure was credited to `a`. Fixes and results for one key also attach to the other through
  `ContainsKey`, so one key's fix can discharge another key's failure.
- Why: blocker-grade under the brief ("a chunk that can fall out of the union"). Upstream
  `scripts/lib/rc-incremental-plan.ps1:44,80,135` keys the universe and launch set Ordinal, so
  case-only-distinct keys are legitimate there. The plan states canonical keys and Ordinal order.
- Fix: use `[Collections.Generic.Dictionary[string,object]]::new([StringComparer]::Ordinal)` for
  `$states`. Add a witness with two case-only-distinct keys: both are in `launch`, and a failure or
  fix on one does not move the other's band.

- Where: `scripts/lib/rc-dirty-order.ps1:85` (`Carry` goes to `omitted` before any history check),
  and `:128` (results for keys without a state are skipped).
- Failure: the obligations are `k` Carry and `d` Dirty. The attempts are `k` failed at sha A, then
  `k` passed at the same sha A with `fixProven=true`. The probe output is `launch=d:not-run omitted=k`.

- Why: blocker-grade under the brief: a chunk with an undischarged failure can fall out of what

  `interrupted` (for example `throw 'carry-with-open-failure'`) rather than adding it, so
  U = D ∪ S ∪ (C − S) stays exact. Add a witness with a Carry key after a failure plus a same-tip
  or FixProven green.
