## D-1 (measured, blocking under the frozen budget): Linux tick-p95 miss at load

**Where:** `tests/Antiphon.SessionRunner.Tests/C1514HostLoadNativeOverheadTests.cs:34`, `Linux_SamplerOverheadStaysWithinBudget`, run `20261107-155611-da81` row CP-6 at `6e21459f7`.

**Failure:** `on.P95TickMs should be less than 25d but was 25.687285000000003d` on pair 0 after the CPU delta and sample-count assertions passed. Row wall 163 s, slot granted 16:07:05, 1-min load 20-26 on 24 cores while two foreign whole-Unit lanes ran (`task-933d9118`, `task-47538c88`).

**Why:** The CPU budget is met, but the tick budget is not robust: with 12-13 ticks `ticks[ceil(.95*n)-1]` is the maximum, so one late full-opt JIT tick on the sampling thread (tiered compilation is off in the children) decides the row. The Code's two passing samples ran at load 5-9. The test design says a measured failure goes to Debug with its host load and must not be repeated until green, so this row was not rerun; load was 40-76 for the rest of the review.

**Fix:** Debug with this run, source and load. Keep `< 25` ms and `< 1` point; make the tick sample a real p95 (enough ticks that the single worst tick is excluded, or a longer measured window), warm the late paths before the measured window, and record ambient load in the receipt. Do not widen the limit or add a retry.
