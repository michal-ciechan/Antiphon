## Defects

### D-1 (Medium, adequacy): the first-pass zero-row protection is unwitnessed, and the "zero-row-recheck" arm does not test the recheck

Where:
- `server/Application/Services/AgentTaskDispatcher.cs:9334` (`missingTurnBoundary.Contains(sid)` in `protectedAgents`).
- `:9442` (`recheckMissingBoundary.Contains(sid)`).
- `tests/Antiphon.Tests/Application/TaskSupervisionVisibilityTests.cs` ZeroRowRecheckAsync, which calls only `SessionsWithoutTurnBoundaryAsync`.

Failure: removing the first-pass clause keeps all 20 committed C1610 arms green, because the recheck still refuses the kill. But the behaviour regresses in two ways:
1. Cap: with 4 warm agents and cap 3, where the oldest has zero rows and the rest have a TurnEnd, the tip retires the next-oldest proved-idle agent. Without the clause nothing is retired and the cap is breached (rv-mixed-cap).
2. Stale rows: a warm agent whose session is already Stopped with zero rows, past TTL, is swept as stale at the tip. Without the clause it is retained at the recheck on every sweep and the row never goes (rv-zero-terminal).

Separately, the recheck clause survives its own removal (25/25 green). The arm named zero-row-recheck witnesses the helper, not the call site.

Why: this clause is the round-2 D-1 fix site, and this is the same class as round-2 D-2 (a clause masked by its sibling).

- Add two V-1 arms with exactly the probe shapes above. In mixed-cap, assert the oldest zero-row agent is not killed and the 2-minute TurnEnd agent is killed with its row removed. In zero-row-terminal, use SeedWarmAsync(status: Stopped) and assert the agent row is removed.
- Then either add a seam that witnesses the recheck clause, or say in the plan that the recheck clause is unwitnessed defence in depth and rename or describe zero-row-recheck as a helper witness.
