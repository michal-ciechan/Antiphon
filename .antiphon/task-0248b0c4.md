## Confirmed sound (priority 1, 2 and 4)

**Lock order:**
- Cold launch locks X (`LockPhysicalAgentAsync` `:6143`) before `RefuseParallelAsync`, then
  flushes, consumes and commits: 1->2->3->5->6->flush->7->8.
- Warm reuse takes the previous owner (3), S `KEY SHARE` (4), then X `FOR UPDATE` with a reload
  (5), all before rank 6.
- Standing placement takes 4 before 6.
- Reroute owns its transaction: task `FOR UPDATE`, recheck, `ClaimLost`, then the ambient
  supersede (3->7).
- Routing resume uses one transaction per task.
- The continuation takes A->R->S->X (`AgentTaskService.cs:3635-3649`).

**Rules and amendments:**
- Rule P: the dirty-tracker assertion runs at `RedeemOnAsync(consume:true)` entry, right after
  the flush, and V-9 `claim-launch` checks it. It is real and on the right line.
- Rule F: every existing row is pre-locked before rank 6.
- F3: `ReloadAsync` runs after `TakeProviderLockAsync`, in both `RedeemOnAsync` and
  `ReleaseClaimReservationOnAsync`.

**Exactly-once admission:**
- The consume and the receipt are in one commit. A refusal abandons the claim.
- A lost acknowledgement is reread. Enqueue failure (V-6) and a crash after commit (V-5) each
  consume once.
- The gate runs `consume:false` on its own scope.
- Non-launch commits release `claim-not-launched` in the claim transaction.
- The D-9 chain walk sees `PostgresException` both inside `DbUpdateException` and raw.

**Rollback helper:** `AbandonClaimAsync` snapshots the non-`Added` tasks, agents and sessions,
calls `Clear()`, and reloads them in place. Reloading adopts committed values, and the tick loop
saves immediately, so it cannot drop a concurrent change. V-7 is green.

**V-14:** the expectations follow the plan's V-14 text and D-9 class 2, and tick 2 proves a
single admission. That is the planned contract, not new behaviour being blessed.
