## What changed

D-1. `c1609_apply_gate` now runs inside `c1008_recycle` immediately after context validation, and only when the entry is not a dry run. It is before the protected-name check, the lock, any journal write, `docker stop`, `docker rm`, preview consume, and `docker volume rm`. A missing or non-matching token calls `write_result false 'apply-gate-off' 2` and does not call `c1008_refuse`. Default redeploy-old does not recycle: it prints `C1008_RECYCLE skipped=redeploy-old reason=not-requested` and runs deploy-parent with no recycle. `-RecycleVolumes` is the explicit recycle, and it is refused unless the phase is `redeploy-old` or `all`. `-DryRun` does not hit the gate and does not require `-RecycleVolumes`.

D-2. The same early gate means a refused temp apply does not mark the Ready preview consumed. A later token apply can still remove the four targets. Gate 9 says the preview expires after 900 s, so the second rollout needs a fresh `-DryRun` and an apply within 15 minutes.

Protected names are refused at target selection, diagnosis `RecycleProtectedVolume`, through `write_result`, before any delete. Temp `antiphon-runner-temp_runner-state` stays a legitimate delete target.
