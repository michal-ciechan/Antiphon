(b) The window never opens earlier for an active session, and skew cannot open it. Old rule: `first + StableFor >= CreatedAt + 120 s`, with `first` the runner's wall clock. New rule: `StableFor >= 120 s` and `observedAt - CreatedAt >= 120 s`, both durations on their own clock, no cross-clock subtraction.
