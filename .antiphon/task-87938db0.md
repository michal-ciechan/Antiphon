## Rule now in force (DispatchConcurrencyEditor.tsx)
- **Snapshot identity.** Query snapshots are compared by data object and `dataUpdatedAt` (`after-failed-refresh-equal-data`).
- **Not adopted.** `superseded`: the cache state read with `getQueryState` after `mutateAsync` resolves (`S2F2 refresh-fails`, `refresh-pending`). S1's `onSuccess` calls `invalidateQueries` before `mutateAsync` resolves and does not await the GET.
- **Adopted.** A snapshot not yet seen and not `superseded`, with no revision-order comparison (`rollback-*`, `reused-revision`, `same-revision-new-content`, `after-save-lower`).
- **Save reply.** `settleSave` keeps the reply unless `isOlder(reply, base)`; `base` can be the pre-submit load, a read during the PUT or an earlier Save reply. Tested with an increasing counter (`late-put-newer-get`); restore during the PUT: see Residuals.
- **Changed document.** `adoptServer` treats a document as changed when its revisions, its mode or a cell differ (`same-revision-new-content`).
- **Dirty draft (D-7).** On a changed document, `rebaseDraft` keeps cells that differ from the old saved state; the other cells and the mode come from the new document; the banner lists remaining differences (`rollback-dirty-*`).
- **Conflict reload.** Goes through `adoptServer` with the banner forced on (`V12 conflict-*`, `conflict-reload-controls`).
