## Advisories (non-blocking)
- **A-1:** the late-report latency bound under a large due backlog is the formula in check 1, not a constant. Consider a targeted pass on transcript growth, or a separate scan budget, if Pending undeliverable debt grows.
- **A-2:** the no-growth signal is `max(Sequence)`. Out-of-order backfill below the max would not reset the drain. The report itself is still found while the watch is open, because an ended scan bypasses the frontier early-return. This only shortens the window if such backfill exists.
- **A-3:** a source that is Stopped but keeps growing keeps its watch open, retention-protected, at 60-min scans. This is bounded work, but there is no ceiling on it.
- **A-4:** if a watch has reached 60-min backoff, the effective drain runs about 75 min (first post-exit scan) plus 60 more. That is safe, but longer than the documented "15 minutes" suggests.
