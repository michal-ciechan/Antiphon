## Timestamp set

Accepted (exactly the documented set):
- `yyyy-MM-ddTHH:mm:ssZ`, with 1 to 7 fractional digits
- 0001-01-01 through 9999-12-31T23:59:59.9999999Z
- a JSON-escaped `T`. JSON decodes it to `T` before parsing, so it is the same instant.

Refused on all three fields (the exact-Full anchorCompletedAtUtc, a chain anchor, and utcNow):
- leap second `23:59:60` (on Linux), `24:00`, `:60` minutes or seconds, month 13, Feb 30
- a lowercase `t` or `z`, a full-width `Ｚ`, Arabic-Indic digits
- offsets `+00:00`, `-00:00`, `+0000`, `+00:00Z`; `GMT`; RFC1123 form
- leading or trailing whitespace, a space before `Z`, `\n`, an embedded NUL
- 8 fractional digits, `.Z`, a comma fraction, `.-1`
- year 0000, year 10000, `+2026`, two-digit years and single-digit fields
- date-only, time-only, date-less `00:00Z`, no seconds, a local (no Z) time, a space separator
- the basic format, week dates and ordinal dates

**Round-1 A-2 decision.** An exact Full dated in the far future (9999-12-31) still answers `full`, and so does one with a null time. Neither is a blocker for the predicate. The exact-Full verdict never consults the time; the doc says there is no age limit. Every other exact-Full field (admitted, allExecuted, carriedCount) is trusted from the same admitted envelope. A writer who can forge the time can therefore forge the verdict outright, so refusing a future time adds consistency, not safety. I still recommend refusing a future-dated or time-less exact Full as defence in depth. Advisory A-2, for a card.

## Advisories (cards, not land holds)

- **A-1. Reason naming.** `full-anchor-future` and `full-anchor-time-unknown` contain `full`. Rename them to `anchor-future` and `anchor-time-unknown`, or document that callers must compare `Kind == "full"` exactly.
- **A-2. Exact Full time.** An exact Full with a future (or null) `anchorCompletedAtUtc` answers `full`. Recommend refusing it as defence in depth.
- **A-3. Constructors.** `RcTagBackingResult` has a public constructor and a public `Chain(...)` factory. Any future caller can mint a `chain-to-full` result without `Test`. Make them internal or private.
- **A-4. Deep-nesting witness.** `DeepNestingIsChainInvalid` uses depth 32. A depth-10000 case would pin the "no StackOverflow" claim directly. Behaviour is correct today.
- **A-5. Document clock.** `utcNow` comes from the document, not the host clock. The chain form is off, so this matters only once D-8 enables it. The leap-second refusal is observed on Linux only; Windows parse behaviour of `:60` is unobserved.
