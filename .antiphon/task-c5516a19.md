## Priority 2: guarantees and TimeSpan.Parse
- The b71d9b31..HEAD lib delta is exactly two private C# functions (IndexOf and FallbackSeconds). No call site, deadline, cap or open path changed. Round 1 confirmed the post-exit, fail-closed, FIFO, symlink, denied and memory-cap guarantees, and they still hold. The harness rows that witness them pass at head.
- Parser equivalence: 31 duration forms, run through Summarize under de-DE and under tr-TR, give byte-identical output from the b71d9b31 lib and the head lib, and de-DE equals tr-TR. The forms covered:
  - c-format, d.hh:mm:ss.fffffff, negative and bare days
  - comma decimal, leading and trailing spaces
  - TimeSpan.MaxValue and MaxValue+1
  - `10675200:00:00:00`, which overflows in the fallback and is refused
  - `1:2:3:4` (d:h:m:s), `1:2:3:4:5`, `+00:00:01`, `1e3`, an Arabic-Indic digit, `-`, `.5`, `1.`, `00:00:01Z`, `I:00:00`

## Advisories
- A-1: the denylist in `ps51-add-type-netfx-clean` gives limited assurance.
  - Probed C# 6+ constructs it does NOT flag: `=>` members, auto-property initializers, exception filters, the `default` literal, `is T x`, `using static`, local functions, throw expressions, digit separators, `Array.Empty`, `Math.Clamp`, `Contains(string, StringComparison)`, `AsSpan()`, deconstruction, ref locals, `^1`, `..`, target-typed `new()` and `??=`.
  - It scans only `Add-Type -TypeDefinition @'...'@` forms in two files, so it misses `-TypeDefinition $var`, `-Path x.cs` and a `-ReferencedAssemblies`-first form.
  - Suggested: compile each extracted block with the SDK's Roslyn csc at `-langversion:5`, which is on every runner. Keep the API denylist or add a reference allowlist.
  - Its `TimeSpan.TryParse-provider` rule and the code comment are factually wrong: `TimeSpan.TryParse(string, IFormatProvider, out TimeSpan)` exists in .NET Framework 4.0+. The change is harmless.
- A-2: `five-thousand-result-trx-streams-inside-budget` no longer witnesses throughput.
  - Mutation M3 slowed the scanner (`SpinWait(16000)` per tag), taking the 5000-result read from 26 to 2357 ms. The whole harness stayed GREEN (70/0): the stats came from the 10 s injected read, and the bounded read refused at 218 ms, which the new `wideClosed` accepts.
  - So a 20 to 90x scan regression, which would make production refuse every TRX of that size, passes.
  - This is what round 1's D-2 fix asked for, so it is not a defect. Consider bounding the injected read too (for example under 2x the largest p95 sample, provisional), or a MEASURED trend gate.
- A-3: the PS5.1 native witness remains pending. It is named, skipped on Linux, and not discharged.
- A-4 (inherited): `post-run-hostile-files-keep-exit-inside-budget` asserts total aggregation under 0.2 s, at the exact budget. The row is unchanged from base and has the same shape as D-2.
  - It went red in checkpoint run d0f1 under load 15 to 20, and green on the rerun.
  - Hostile-path probe, sequential, fresh roots, 15 runs per lib at load about 4: base 50 to 460 ms (first call 355 and 460 ms); head 51 to 183 ms (first call 73 and 79 ms, helped by the warmup).
  - Head is no slower than base, so this is not a regression, but the row flakes on loaded runners. Same fix as D-2: separate the bound from correctness, or use a 2x margin.
  - The `post-run-hostile-files` row also went red under M2, but that is the same timing flake: its TRX is a FIFO and is never scanned.
