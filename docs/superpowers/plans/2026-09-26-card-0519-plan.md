# CARD-0519: recover outbound publication without replaying an ambiguous send

Date: 2026-09-26. Stage: Investigate/Plan. Inspected `origin/master` at
`4e873cf20a61b0475f9a013b57168e6ecdd7aea5` and CARD-0418's Review branch
`origin/feat/card-task-7a479eaf` at `0b314a9eaa42ea5865c1f84fc39d67ab2067ebd8`.
CARD-0519 is a follow-on to CARD-0418: **the outbound delivery entity and pump do not
exist on master yet**. Land/rebase CARD-0418 first, then recheck these line references
before coding. CARD-0593 is the analogous *inbound* loss path and remains a separate card.
No source or tests were changed or run in this investigation.

## Verified findings

1. The card's present-tense claim is stale **on master**: `server/Application/Services/ChannelReplyDispatcher.cs:427,1161,1333` still calls the messaging producer directly; master has no `ChannelOutboundDelivery` or `ChannelOutboundService`. The issue is valid on CARD-0418's Review branch, not already fixed. Its current tests explicitly pin the gaps rather than proving the intended behavior.
2. On that branch, `server/Application/Services/ChannelOutboundService.cs:102-111` selects only Pending/Converting/Ready. At `:517-548` it commits Publishing, calls `IAntiphonMessagingProducer.SendAsync`, and maps any non-cancellation exception to PublishUncertain until the configured attempt cap. `server/Application/Settings/ChannelOutboundSettings.cs:20,46-47` sets/validates `MaxPublishAttempts=3`, but a refused row is never selected again, making the cap unreachable in ordinary execution. `tests/Antiphon.Tests/Application/ChannelOutboundDeliveryTests.cs:412-469` asserts precisely that one-attempt behavior. The card's word *silently* is too broad for this case: `server/Application/Services/AttentionService.cs:2826-2848` already shows PublishUncertain, with only OpenDrawer as its action. The refused reply still does not get delivered.
3. `ChannelOutboundService.cs:517-521` persists Publishing before the producer call. A process death at either side of broker acceptance leaves it there; the pump's selection above and `AttentionService.cs:2828-2831` both exclude Publishing. `tests/Antiphon.Tests/Application/ChannelOutboundRecoveryTests.cs:174-239` kills a real child at C-6 (before producer call) and C-7 (after acceptance), then asserts the row remains Publishing through three fresh processes with no attention recovery. This is the genuinely silent failure.
4. `src/Antiphon.Messaging.Client/IAntiphonMessagingProducer.cs:5-8` returns only `Task`; `KafkaAntiphonMessagingProducer.cs:26-34` discards `ProduceAsync`'s delivery result. The spy's `OutboundDefiniteNonAcceptanceException` is test-only (`tests/Antiphon.Tests/TestHelpers/OutboundProducerSpy.cs:60-76,143`), so production has no trusted nonacceptance signal. Confluent's [PersistenceStatus contract](https://docs.confluent.io/platform/current/clients/confluent-kafka-dotnet/_site/api/Confluent.Kafka.PersistenceStatus.html) says NotPersisted cannot duplicate on retry, while PossiblyPersisted can. A `ProduceException` carries a [DeliveryResult](https://docs.confluent.io/platform/current/clients/confluent-kafka-dotnet/_site/api/Confluent.Kafka.ProduceException-2.html). An arbitrary exception, timeout, cancellation, or a failed DB save after broker acceptance cannot prove nonacceptance.

## Smallest fix

Keep the current delivery states and Kafka transport. No automatic replay of
PublishUncertain, including C-6 where no call actually happened: that fact is not
durably distinguishable from C-7 after a crash.

**S1 — producer outcome contract.** Add a narrow public exception or result at the
`IAntiphonMessagingProducer` boundary that means *broker definitely did not persist
this record*. `KafkaAntiphonMessagingProducer` emits it only for a
`ProduceException<string,string>` with a non-null delivery result whose `Status`
is `NotPersisted`, or a proven local pre-enqueue rejection. Preserve every other
failure as ambiguous. Keep `Task SendAsync` compatibility if possible by using a
typed exception, and update the test spy to emit that production type. Test the
real mapper with NotPersisted, PossiblyPersisted, missing result, and successful
acknowledgement; do not infer safety from `Error.IsRetriable` alone. Preserve the
existing key/topic and serialized bytes.

**S2 — bounded safe retry and stamps.** Before each producer call, persist
`PublishAttempts` and Publishing so a crash cannot erase an attempted call.
Catch the typed nonacceptance around the producer call alone; after a definite
refusal, persist Ready for the next 2-second pump tick, clear the lease, and keep
the sealed payload/hash. At `MaxPublishAttempts`, persist Failed with bounded
reason and attention. Any other exception becomes PublishUncertain immediately.
An acknowledgement followed by a DB/stamp failure must remain recoverably
uncertain, never count as nonacceptance. Published and correlation/deliverable
stamps occur only after acknowledged publication. Recheck binding before each
retry. No converter rerun and no retry on Failed/PublishUncertain.

**S3 — crash recovery and visibility.** After lease expiry, let the pump claim a
Publishing row using its existing version/lease compare-and-swap, then transition
it to PublishUncertain with `publish-interrupted` evidence and clear the lease.
Fence the former owner: its late completion must not overwrite a recovered row
or settle correlations unless its original claim version still owns Publishing.
If a producer call outlives its lease, conservative uncertainty is acceptable;
automatic replay is not. PublishUncertain appears on attention with delivery,
channel, task, age, and a bounded reason. An expired Publishing row should also
be visible directly until recovery succeeds, so a broken pump cannot hide it.
Document operator inspection in `docs/telegram.md`: inspect broker/gateway
evidence before any deliberate resend; an uncertain row offers no automatic
retry or success stamp. This card does not claim exactly-once gateway delivery.

Existing no-profile direct sends are outside this durable conversion queue and
remain outside this change. CARD-0593's inbound wake failure is not addressed here.

## Risks and limits

- A broker acceptance can precede a crash or a DB failure. Without a durable
  broker receipt joined to this delivery ID, automatic replay of such a row can
  duplicate a user-visible message. The plan deliberately leaves ambiguous
  rows for operator investigation.
- A slow but live producer can outlast a lease. The version fence must stop its
  late database write. It may have accepted the message; attention must say
  uncertain rather than claim failure or success.
- CARD-0418 is not on master. Its Review branch is far ahead of its merge base;
  inspect the landed code before implementing this plan. A landing that already
  resolves these gaps should close CARD-0519 after evidence, rather than apply
  this plan mechanically.

## Verification design

- **V-1 (producer truth):** NotPersisted becomes the typed definite refusal; PossiblyPersisted, missing/unknown status, timeout and cancellation never do. Success remains success. Verify actual `KafkaAntiphonMessagingProducer` classification, not only a fake's exception.
- **V-2 (refusal retry):** Two definite refusals then one acceptance produce one accepted record, three attempts, identical sealed bytes, and one settlement. Three definite refusals produce Failed/attention and no stamp. A subsequent pump makes no fourth call. A late profile revocation holds the delivery before the next call.
- **V-3 (ambiguous outcome):** Accepted-then-thrown and producer/DB post-ack failure remain PublishUncertain; repeated ticks cause no additional accepted record or false stamp. Failure before the producer call can still be uncertain if it was persisted as Publishing.
- **V-4 (hard crash/fencing):** Update C-6/C-7 real killed-process test to expect PublishUncertain plus attention after lease expiry, with zero and one accepted records respectively, and no second record after repeated fresh-process ticks. Exercise a live slow producer whose lease expires and assert that late completion cannot overwrite recovery or stamp success.
- **V-5 (attention):** Expired Publishing is visible before recovery, PublishUncertain and Failed remain visible after it, active unexpired Publishing does not alarm, and Published has no delivery-fault item. No read mutates the row. Preserve frozen routing and conversion-task references.

`ChannelOutboundAttentionTests` is a new focused class. Existing delivery and
recovery classes have baseline tests on CARD-0418; their current-outcome
assertions must be replaced with predicted-baseline-red behavior checks.
TestDesign should assign explicit method names, red controls, and a fresh-TRX
roster without expanding this closed class list. Do not count internal assertions
as executions. The CP-3 hard-kill lane already uses an assembly-local process
spawn limiter.
