Plan complete under the defaults below. Fix hour-only reset parsing and stale hold repair, then add durable capacity-wait bookkeeping to the existing recovery, queue, supervisor and dispatcher. Both expiry and deliberate clear make still-authorized blocked work eligible for paced recovery. Delivery and useful progress remain separate evidence. Implementation and verification have not run; TestDesign is the next stage.

## Reset parsing and repair contract

### Source-time parsing

`UsageLimitWallParser` remains pure. Rename/document its time argument as evidence time rather than wall-clock now. Parse `You've hit your session limit · resets 9am (Europe/London)` and the `2pm` variant, `reset at 9 AM`, and existing `6:10pm`, `09:00` formats. Hour-only without AM/PM is deliberately outside the new grammar; preserve the documented minute-bearing 24-hour format. Omitted minutes mean zero only in the valid hour-only branch.

Use named regex groups and token boundaries so `9:7am`, `9:000am`, `9amjunk`, `25:00`, `0am`, `13pm` and a malformed supplied zone cannot partially match a shorter valid prefix. Unknown zones and invalid times degrade to ModelCap with an explicit parse-failure diagnostic; distinguish supplied-but-invalid reset from no reset stated in the reason. Preserve alias normalization, named-model extraction, structural Wall classification and Grok credit vocabulary.

Persist `EvidenceAt` and its source on the recovery at first adoption: TurnEnd.Timestamp, else its matched AssistantText.Timestamp, else TurnEnd.CreatedAt, else initial DetectedAt. Null/unusable timestamps fall through this order; enrichment can improve a fallback to a real provider timestamp once, recording the change, but later sweeps never substitute their current time. Pass these facts through `ApiErrorStubText`, adoption and the task reply arm; there must not be two different reset calculations for one stub.

Compute the next occurrence at-or-after that evidence instant in the named zone, preserving current calendar semantics. Thus a source timestamp already past the stated local clock can legitimately mean the next day; this change does not invent a missing provider date. London DST conversion is retained; reject nonexistent local times, choose the later UTC occurrence for an ambiguous local time and pin that conservative policy in tests. No zone means UTC. A delayed repair whose computed deadline is already past clears through the normal expiry transition immediately; it does not roll forward from repair time. The no-reset fallback is `EvidenceAt + EffectiveModelCapFallbackHoldHours`, not each re-adoption's now. HitAt remains write/observation history; it is not the parser clock.

### Exact, idempotent enrichment

Persist on `ApiErrorRecovery` the hold Id/revision it applied, evidence timestamp/source, parse version and the digest/status of the evidence applied. Persist on the active hold its latest evidence recovery Id. Evidence enrichment is an update of that relationship, not a call to the general hold upsert.

An enrichment transaction must prove all of the following:

1. The exact `(sessionId, stubSequence)` recovery exists and is structurally Wall. New text improves empty evidence or is a one-time parser-version correction of the known hour-only failure.
2. Its linked hold is still uncleared and matches the expected revision and latest evidence recovery. Both alias and kind match. A different session overwriting the hold evidence invalidates the old writer.
3. After transcript catch-up, this remains the relevant latest wall and no later real/queued accepted prompt or completion has taken over. Suppression must use the established transcript-kind helpers, excluding local commands, synthetic compaction continuation and housekeeping.
4. No terminal task/cancellation/reroute, manual takeover or completed recovery has superseded it. Legacy WallModelPaused/WallParked may permit evidence-only correction under the compatibility rules; they do not gain a task retry merely by changing text.

Missing hold, cleared hold, revision mismatch, changed source, no improvement or ambiguous legacy identity means no mutation. A cleared row's ClearedAt, clear cause and original resolution history never change. Do not replace `ResolvedAt` on every sweep. A legitimate new structural wall after progress may create a new hold Id; the old stub cannot.

Refactor new adoption so the recovery, hold association and capacity-wait registration commit atomically. `BuildNewRowAsync` currently causes hold saves before the recovery insert; that ordering cannot be the new lineage contract. Allocate the recovery Id first, insert/link in one transaction, and distinguish unique-key adoption races from unrelated DbUpdateExceptions. The auto writer must enlist in the caller's transaction. Explicit model APIs still own their transaction. Manual precedence applies to enrichment as well: no deadline shortening or demotion; a later Manual PUT invalidates earlier evidence revisions.

The relevance check also applies to a previously **unadopted** historical stub: if catch-up already proves a later accepted prompt, successful completion or newer wall, persist only its Superseded/Replaced audit recovery and do not write a hold. Otherwise fixing the existing-row repair branch would leave an equivalent resurrection through first adoption after delayed ingestion.

### Clear and consume

All clear paths call one transactional primitive: explicit DELETE, timed sweep, lazy lookup/list/snapshot, legacy AutoDetected-null normalization and a correction whose deadline has passed. It stamps ClearedAt, ClearCause and ReleasePendingAt exactly once. Repeated clears are no-ops. Concurrent clear/upsert/repair uses the same kind/hold serialization and revision checks; a committed clear cannot be lost by a stale tracked entity.

The existing supervisor hosted loop consumes pending transitions at startup/first tick and periodically. It durably marks linked waits for reevaluation and acknowledges the release in the same database transaction; it performs no input or launch in that transaction. Mark ReleaseConsumedAt only after all relevant links have been processed, in bounded batches with a durable cursor if needed. Registering a wait after its hold cleared must immediately observe the clear; it cannot depend on an event that has already been consumed. Pending waits also reevaluate effective availability periodically, so no transient publish failure can strand them.

Eligibility always calls the current alias + kind-wide availability policy. A cleared specific hold with an active `*`, or a replacement hold after a clear, does not authorize a launch. Register the newly observed blocking generation and defer. Routing waits rerun the existing candidate walk; a stale chosen alias is not authority. Distinct stored clear causes describe the holds that released this work, including both causes when overlapping holds clear differently.

No IEventBus publish, queue flush, transcript RPC or Start runs under these database transactions. Publish only after commit; failure leaves durable state for the next tick. Use a consistent database lock order across the new paths: provider state, affected consumer owner (agent/task/session), wait, then hold rows in stable key order. Refactor existing supervisor/adoption transactions where necessary rather than acquiring the provider lock inside an already inverted order. Existing per-session queue locks continue to serialize composer access; no code acquires that semaphore while holding the database locks.

### Episode/action state

`WaitingForHold -> Ready -> ActionPending -> Admitted -> PromptConfirmed -> Progressed` is the normal live-session path. A launch can record `StartAccepted` and then `Running` before its useful prompt is confirmed. A task dispatch records its accepted attempt and then boot-prompt confirmation. These intermediate outcomes do not claim useful work completed.

Side outcomes are `Deferred` (busy, active hold, unavailable backend or temporary prerequisite), `Superseded`/`Canceled`, `Reheld`, and `Exhausted`. Admission and action transitions compare the wait version; a late callback for an earlier ordinal can enrich that action's evidence but cannot release the current barrier. No unresolved recovery callback can overwrite a newer task attempt.

Every action key derives from `(waitId, actionOrdinal)`. A new ordinal is issued only after a recorded outcome requires another capacity attempt, never on a polling tick or merely because a lease expired. Once side effects may have occurred, recovery inspects the existing queue/launch/dispatch receipt first. An unknown result does not mean create another action. It becomes a visible unresolved outcome handled by existing delivery/launch reconciliation.

### Queue and terminal barrier

Keep NeedsHuman, AuthenticationRequired/provider sign-in, UnknownExhausted and WallUnparsed outside capacity auto-resume. Herdr blocked/unreachable, rules initialization/refresh failures, delivery-attempt caps and forbidden/truncated bodies remain authoritative queue barriers.

Do not change `HasTerminalCapacityHoldAsync` to simply return false whenever the model is available. Select exactly one recovery action under the queue's per-session lock after catch-up and stale-message checks. Prefer the oldest still-eligible Channel/Scheduled row; preserve existing contiguous same-conversation batching as one submission. A newer schedule copy cancels the old selection and causes reselection before input. If no queued work exists and the interrupted turn/refused dispatch is still relevant, enqueue the appropriate short Supervision continuation with the action key and `deliverIfIdle: false`.

The ordinary flush is the final authority: verify owner/version and current availability, then acquire admission immediately before durable delivery-start intent. Only the selected message/batch may pass its linked capacity barrier. Other capacity-blocked messages remain pending until that action is confirmed and a normal turn boundary allows the next delivery. Fresh holds and new terminal walls close it again; non-capacity barriers are never cleared by this acknowledgement. UI/manual input retains its existing semantics and can supersede an untyped automatic action.

Persist queue row creation/selection with the wait state, using an enlistable queue operation or keyed enqueue that can safely be rediscovered after save failure. Keep the queue's baseline, complete prompt matcher, late confirmation and LF/bracketed-paste/separate-Enter transport unchanged. A new action cannot reset DeliveryAttempts or retype a parked/truncated message. Crash after input but before acknowledgement reconciles the same message against the transcript. Enqueued or screen-only Delivered is logged as unconfirmed, never `Auto-resumed`.

For later UserPrompt/QueuedUserPrompt evidence, first determine whether it is the chosen action's accepted body. That body confirms the action; unrelated genuine input suppresses the stale continuation. Ignore local commands and compaction housekeeping for useful-progress decisions. A failed catch-up is Deferred, never evidence of absence. Recheck at final flush, because a human can continue after scheduling and before delivery.

### Supervision and launch

Handle ModelDisabledException before the generic failure arm. Neither first refusal nor repeated hold observations increase ConsecutiveFailures, escalation tiers or capacity attempt count. Store capacity timing separately from pre-existing crash NextRestartAt; on release pull forward only delay attributable to capacity. Preserve real crash backoff and Herdr streaks, including typed asynchronous launch failures.

Add an internal automatic-start context carrying the wait/action and expected owner/session generation. In `AgentControlService`, recheck Suspended, LivenessLatchedAt, HerdrFailureHeldAt, AlwaysOn, current ownership and terminal/canceled card intent under the agent transaction before committing a start. This path must not call the human latch-clear behavior. Keep public manual Start/explicit Herdr retry behavior intact. A Stop racing admission wins if committed before start intent; a Stop after an accepted start follows the existing stop/launch-cancellation path. Capacity actions never set ResetHerdrFailureHold or AllowUnauthenticatedProvider.

The standard AlwaysOn `IgnoreSubscriptionQuota: true` precedent is retained for this same caller only; create/dispatch quota policy and explicit routing overrides are not widened. Authentication, rules, placement and model Require guards still run. A 409 for another barrier records/delegates to that barrier's existing handling; it is not automatically retried by spending the capacity budget every minute.

Use normal native resume when ProviderContractCatalog and the existing session identity allow it. Capacity-only failures cannot cross FreshAfterResumeFailures. Existing rules migration/resume-not-found refusals and unsupported/unknown native resume contracts remain visible decisions; never silently replay an unfinished task into a fresh context. An AlwaysOn owner with no interrupted conversation can still perform its ordinary authorized initial start. An actual independent crash recovery may keep its existing Fresh policy, with its cause recorded separately.

Persist the action reference on the accepted launch/session incarnation before async launch, so a restart finds the prior request and never launches twice because NextRestartAt survived. Tie the launch's first useful input to this action. Coordinate `DeliverLaunchNoteAsync`, `EnqueueResumeContinueAsync`, pending channel work and the boot-probe branch: mandatory rules initialization retains priority, but only one selected work continuation is emitted for the capacity action. A queued real message or existing interrupted-turn continue covers it. Do not add a WallPrompt after the launch path already resumed the work. Running/ready alone can complete a pure process-availability wait, but must not be described as a confirmed resumed work turn.

### Tasks and existing dispatch protections

Run current declared routing first. An allowed alternate remains allowed; Required pins, forbidden aliases, cost ceilings, workspace scope, specialist limits, manual card moves and cascade exhaustion retain their precedence. A task already requeued by routing has one dispatcher wait, not a second same-session recovery. Preserve the existing Rerouted event/cascade accounting; do not zero its count to obtain a capacity retry.

For future canonical Wall cases whose current live task remains resumable on its existing session, leave Status=Working with `CapacityWaitId` and a typed wait reason; do not emit Failed or release its delegate at ModelCap merely because the old recovery reason was terminal. Recovery resolution and capacity waiting are separate concepts. Until the episode is exhausted, show that it is waiting for capacity and the next due time. Non-capacity failures keep their current settlement behavior.

Retained tasks keep their workspace lease and count toward existing occupied-task limits; resuming the same task does not consume a second slot or conflict with its own lease. Pausing six dirty Shared tasks may constrain throughput: releasing ownership to improve that is intentionally not part of this change. Update overdue/check/settlement paths to defer only the typed eligible wait; existing specialist execution deadlines and cancellation still win. On exhaustion, use existing visible fail/release behavior and dirty-work diagnostics; leave the process killed, pooled warm or standing-owned. If an ephemeral session dies, do not create a fresh retry from its old work merely because a hold cleared.

## Pacing and retry policy

Add `Supervision:CapacityRecovery` typed settings with enabled default true after all slices are integrated, `AdmissionIntervalSeconds=60`, `JitterSeconds=30`, `MaxEpisodeAttempts=3`, and a bounded reconciliation batch size of 100. Validate ranges (interval 1-3600, jitter 0-300, attempts 1-10, batch 1-1000) and never permit a zero/unbounded loop. Keep existing Supervision/ApiErrorRecovery disable semantics; capacity actions honor the relevant parent enable flags. Parser correctness and durable clears do not depend on the new enable flag.

Stop using `ApiErrorRecovery:WallDeathCap` as a lifetime row-count gate. When MaxEpisodeAttempts is unspecified, use the existing configured WallDeathCap as its validated compatibility default (normally three); an explicit new value wins. Document the old key as deprecated rather than silently discarding a deployment's chosen bound. Existing transient/UnknownAttemptCap schedules are unchanged.

The existing hosted loop drives reevaluation every supervisor tick (10 seconds by default); expiry/adoption can retain their current minute cadence. Inject TimeProvider into the touched clock decisions/timer so tests do not depend on DateTime.UtcNow. Run expiry/transition reconciliation before capacity candidates and supervisor starts on a tick. Dispatcher and queue checks remain independently correct if they run first or cause lazy expiry.

`EligibleAt = latest required clear/availability-observation time + stableHash(waitId, actionOrdinal) modulo [0, jitterSeconds]`. Apply any later existing crash deadline or execution prerequisite. Among ready consumers choose oldest BlockedAt, then Id. Busy/guarded/ineligible consumers do not reserve future slots or block unrelated ready work. The provider clock is a minimum spacing, not a promise to execute exactly at a scheduled time.

Admission is the durable handoff immediately before the first execution step: queue delivery-start intent, guarded supervised start acceptance, or transactional task dispatch claim. Persist the action/key and `NextAdmissionAt = actualAdmissionTime + interval` together. An enqueue, requeue, skipped gate or prospective due reservation does not spend an attempt. A granted action that never executes may conservatively consume a slot/attempt until its existing receipt is reconciled; it never refunds the clock and releases a burst. Do not pregrant many future slots and then execute all overdue slots after restart.

The launch and its first selected work input share one admission; they do not pay the bucket twice. Long-delayed starts must revalidate an unexecuted admission before beginning, using the same action identity and current provider clock, so boot backlog cannot become a late burst. Transport confirmation/re-Enter for the same queued attempt is not a new capacity retry; any later new-body attempt goes through admission again. The policy bounds Antiphon recovery admissions, not the number of model API calls inside one admitted turn or ordinary new work.

Revalidation of the same accepted action may move its provider clock forward but does not increment its episode attempt count again. A provider-wave revision or newly committed hold invalidates a grant before the first process/input side effect; already-started work is not killed. Recheck and commit intent immediately before the external operation. A hold committed after that linearization point can stop the next action, but cannot retract bytes/RPCs already sent; record that race and let the existing outcome path reconcile it.

On a **new structural wall**, persist the hold before admitting further affected work, increment the provider wave revision and invalidate not-yet-executed reservations. Set the recovery bucket's next possible admission no earlier than wall observation + interval. Reevaluate all pending recovery actions of that kind. Only the affected alias/`*` holds defer until clear; an alias-specific wall must not become an indefinite kind-wide availability hold for other aliases. Unaffected kinds retain independent clocks. Expiry is permission to probe capacity, not proof of capacity.

An episode begins at the first actual capacity block of a logical consumer. It spans successive wall stubs, fallback deadlines, operator clears and supervised incarnations for that same unfinished work. Initial block is attempt zero. Admit up to three attempts; a fourth is Exhausted. A queue acceptance or another wall cannot reset it. A transcript-confirmed successful non-API-error work TurnEnd after the selected prompt closes the session/task episode; mere UserPrompt, local command, unrelated housekeeping, restart uptime or the caller explaining a refused dispatch does not. For a refused-dispatch wait, equivalent successful task creation resolves the intent; for a pure standing process wait, confirmed Running with no outstanding interrupted work resolves it without claiming model success.

After exhaustion no timer or further clear resets that consumer's budget. Existing explicit human continuation/start/task follow-up can recover it; subsequent confirmed useful progress begins a new episode for a later independent wall. No time-of-day or period guessing is needed. A new unrelated task has its own authorization and episode; cascade counters continue to apply separately.

## Compatibility, activation and visibility

Migrations add nullable links/state and constrained defaults only. Do not fabricate historical clear causes or episode successes; already-cleared rows have unknown legacy cause and no pending replay. Do not automatically modify old Failed/Succeeded/Canceled task outcomes. Preserve current active holds, including open-ended Manual ones.
