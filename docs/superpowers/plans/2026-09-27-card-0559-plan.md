## Risks and cost

- The failure must be limited to the one `rev-parse` invocation. If the fake also breaks `show-ref`, the dispatcher skips the sibling at `AgentTaskDispatcher.cs:4227-4228` and the test proves the wrong branch.
- A generic resolver log is possible even under the mutation; the guard must assert the durable sibling intent and Warning event, which identify the omitted branch to the caller.
- A null tip carries no verified commit identity, so the warning correctly says `unknown` and cannot claim how many commits the sibling has. Do not assert a guessed SHA or commit count.
