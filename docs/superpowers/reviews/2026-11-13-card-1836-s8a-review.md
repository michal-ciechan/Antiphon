## Hard checks (brief items 1-5)

1. **Seven-day boundary.** Parent plan D-5 says "age at admission **at least seven days**" forces Full. The code uses `(UtcNow.UtcTicks - completed.UtcTicks) -ge 7 d`.
   - Probes at injected now 2026-11-13T12:00Z:
     - exactly 7 d: expired
     - 7 d minus 1 s: fresh
     - 7 d plus 1 s: expired
     - harness ±1 tick: correct
   - UTC ticks make the comparison independent of offset and DST. A `UtcNow` with a +01:00 or -05:00 offset gives the same verdicts.
   - The ar-SA and th-TH current cultures give the same verdicts.
   - Clock going backwards: the anchor appears to be in the future, which gives `full-anchor-future` and forces Full.
   - Timestamps that give `full-anchor-time-unknown` and force Full:
     - missing or empty
     - `yesterday`
     - no `Z`
     - lowercase `z`
     - `+00:00` or `+01:00` offsets
     - a space separator
     - Arabic-Indic digits
     - `24:00:00`
     - 2026-02-29
     - a `[datetime]` or `[DateTimeOffset]` object
2. **Only an executed Full renews the anchor.** `Get-RcForcedFullDecision` returns the input anchor unchanged. Evidence keys such as `candidateCompletedAtUtc`, `releaseCreatedAtUtc` and `sampleCompletedAtUtc` are ignored, and `kind=carried` gives `full-anchor-unproven`.
   - `Get-RcExecutedFullAnchor` refuses each of these: non-Full mode (shadow, incremental or sample), unverified, incomplete coverage, carries, failed, unexpected skip, membership shortfall (partial or aborted run), malformed or missing counts, origin or time, and a future time.
   - Binding the anchor to the candidate, tree or policy is caller-owned admission. The decision still forces Full on any contract change against the supplied baseline contract.

   - Prior `forcedFull` reasons pass through untouched when a new Full replaces an expired anchor, and they do not veto the new Full (D-5).

  - It fails closed: Full is forced and no anchor is ever accepted.
  - The plan says both functions accept "decoded JSON PSCustomObjects", but the harness only uses hashtables.
  - S5b/S6d/S7b readers must decode with `-DateKind String` (or `-AsHashtable -DateKind String`), and a witness should pin that. Otherwise incremental mode can never leave Full.
