## Cleanup and the charged window

`Remove-C1609LiveScratch` runs after `elapsedMs` is frozen. It tries `[IO.Directory]::Delete` and, on failure, `Remove-Item -LiteralPath -Recurse -Force -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue`. The caller swallows a cleanup error. The coordinator adds each origin's `cleanupMs` back when it computes the next remaining budget and when it checks the window, and it adds the full wall time, including cleanup, to `networkMs`.

Charged work cannot grow past the window by that subtraction. `cleanupMs` is only the delete delta on that origin's stopwatch. Origins run one after another, so the sum of those deltas is a slice of the control stopwatch, not an extra budget. Each origin's own steps still stop at the remaining charged budget. The 600 s network budget still shrinks by cleanup wall time.

Cleanup itself has no timeout. On the fast path the scratch is an empty directory. This round measured cleanup at 13 ms (deadline) and 17 ms (moved-ref). Round 1 measured `Directory.Delete` of that shape at 2 ms, which leaves the 2 s margin under the host's 10 s read. A moved-ref scratch can hold a copied bundle. Its git child is killed at the remaining budget (see below). A delete that fails in both `Directory.Delete` and the `Remove-Item` fallback can leave `c1609-live-<guid>` behind, one new directory per attempt. That is the pre-existing `SilentlyContinue` finally, not a new fail-open. Advisory only.
