## Verdict

Confirmed. Codex 0.160.0 does become ready on this runner. The model footer is not missing and the literal has not changed. A cold start paints the splash at 0.3s and only then spends about 51s reading the local sqlite files and refreshing the model cache before it paints the footer. `CodexReadyMaxWaitMs` is 60000 (`server/Application/Settings/AgentRegistrySettings.cs:63`). Session `e41748c6` was killed at 60.0s while that splash was still the last frame. This cold sample would have latched about 52.5s after the 1s quiet period (`CodexReadyQuietPeriodMs`, `:62`), which is inside 60s, but the failed launch had not finished the same pass at 60s. A warm second launch painted the footer in 1.2s. It is not specific to `gpt-6-astra`.

Smallest remedy: raise `CodexReadyMaxWaitMs` from 60000 to 120000. That covers the measured 51.5s cold paint, the 1s settle, and a pass that was still running at 60s. Not a parser change. Not a re-login.
