## Change

Capture-bearing Outcome notifications discarded the actionable same-card
`unlanded-sibling=<id>:<branch>` marker when replacing publication prose with
compact cleanup diagnostics. `AgentTaskLandService` now carries the marker
separately through terminal settlement to `LandNotificationPayload.Create`.
The compact body places it before diagnostic display text and applies the
existing UTF-8 byte limit to the combined content. Publication prose remains
on the terminal event; notification identity, destination and digest still
bind the complete immutable body.

Eight new producer-to-UserPrompt cases cover busy and already-eligible callers,
modern and inbox delivery profiles, and observed owners versus unavailable
diagnostics. Each creates an actual unlanded same-card Git branch, checks that
the terminal producer emitted the warning, and requires that warning in the
confirmed UserPrompt. The existing eight no-sibling cases also require absence
of the warning. The tests retain complete-body/digest, capture identity,
publication evidence, one queue row/submission, and the 1,024-byte assertion.
They use real Git, isolated PostgreSQL and hosted notification/completion workers;
recipient transport is the established controlled adapter.

The compact-envelope matrix now covers zero, one and two siblings, multibyte
branch/owner text, and equal/distinct approval SHAs. All six cases retain the
capture identity, owner name/PID, Git/probe facts and explicit truncation marker.
