## What was checked

1. **Only the wake worker passes the 1 s bound.** `FlushSessionAsync` / `FlushSessionReportingDeliveryAsync` take an optional `lockWait` that defaults to null, so every existing caller still waits unbounded. The wake worker passes `ScanQueueLockWait` (1 s) in the owned-row sweep and, while the pass is open, in the lane enqueue and the lane flush. A debounced flush after `pass.Close()` keeps the unbounded wait on that lane's own task. Production `DebounceWindowMs` is 500, so the usual enqueue happens on the lane after the pass; lanes do not share one waiter, and the worker loop itself is not on that wait. The sweep, which is the stall site on the worker loop, is bounded.

2. **Order.** A session marked busy has its later rows returned unprocessed, and the member cursor resets, so the next pass starts at the oldest pending row. The order witness releases the lock after the first busy row and asserts the later row was not queued in that pass, then that the next pass queues the earlier row first.

3. **Busy is not a failure.** `SessionQueueBusyException` is caught before the general catch in the lane and in the sweep. The lane catch does not raise the bridge alert. The sweep catch does not fail the pass. Nothing is written when the enqueue wait expires.

4. **Retry and shutdown.** `NextWakeDelay` returns `BusyRetryDelay` (5 s) when any session was skipped, otherwise the 10 s idle tick. The worker loop assigns that value and waits on `Task.Delay` with a token linked to shutdown, or until the wake signal fires, whichever is first. A held lock costs one 1 s wait per session per pass, not a spin.

5. **Heartbeat and stall.** `ChannelInboundWakeSignal` is the singleton both the hosted bridge and `GET /api/channels/wake-worker` use. The route sits on the same `/api/channels` group as `/consumer`, with no extra auth attribute. The body is the pass record (completed time, rows seen, rows queued, sessions skipped, elapsed). It has no message text, credentials, or session ids. The stall check uses the existing `ChannelReplyLost` incident and reason `ChannelWakeWorkerStalled`. It stays quiet while a pass is newer than 5 minutes, and while no deliverable row has been accepted and unqueued for 5 minutes. It fires once per stuck completion time and re-arms when a later pass completes. It is on by default, with the 5-minute ages as constants and no new setting. That is the right shape: the card's failure was a silent stall, and the incident uses the channel incident surface that already exists.
