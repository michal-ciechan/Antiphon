## Recommended fixes

1. Retarget `CodexWorkingIndicator`. The 0.147 line cannot appear in 0.160.0 output. Pin the replacement on one submitted TUI frame (the binary’s run-state words are `Ready`, `Working`, and `Thinking`) and match that line. Until then the screen fallback in `CodexTurnScreenTracker` never completes a turn, and startup never reports `CodexStartupReason.Working`.
2. On that same frame, record the queue-mode footer before changing the `tab to queue message` needle.
3. Leave the normalizer’s switch as it is for this rollout. Correct the class comment that says `codex exec` emits only the flat dialect: this file was thread items with originator `codex_exec`, and the dialect latch already accepted them.
4. Leave the trust detector. Option 2 is no longer `Quit`; the highlight check on `› 1. Trust and continue` still passes.

## Remaining uncertainties

- No TUI rollout was written. The exec file is the live JSONL. The interactive TUI’s `originator` on 0.160.0 was not re-read.
- The in-turn status line and the queue-mode footer were not framed. Their old literals are absent; their replacements are not screenshots.
- `token_count` is still beside the new `token_usage_record`. A later CLI that drops `token_count` would zero the usage stamp.
- Line 0 is 22752 bytes. The 64 KiB lead scan still covers `cwd`. A much larger `base_instructions` would not.
- The signed-out chooser, the update modal, MCP boot, and a usage-limit turn were not on screen. Their literals are in the binary as listed above.
- The `1 warning` chip is the `/tmp` home refusal, not a product modal. Ready classification still held with that chip on the footer.
