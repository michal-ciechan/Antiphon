## Survivors

- **S2 (equivalent).**
  - **Proof:** the total `Clip` only shortens `text` when `text.Length > maxLength`. The loop admits names only while their joined length is ≤ `maxLength - 32`. The suffix `", ... and N more"` is at most 2 + 8 + 10 digits + 5 = 25 < 32 characters.
  - So with any name shown, the text fits. The cut can only fire when no names are shown, and then `text` is the pure-ASCII `"... and N more"`. The step-back never applies at this site.
  - This matches 75c9410c's note that The_list_cap_never_splits_a_surrogate_pair cannot go red.
- **M5 (production-equivalent, contract gap).**
  - **Why it is production-equivalent:** the final cut is reachable only when `maxLength` is below about 23 characters. Every caller passes at least 32: the default is 2000, StageOutcome passes 1000, and MergeRoleDetail floors at 32.
  - **But the contract is broken:** the doc comment promises "never longer than maxLength", and `ConflictFileSummary(names, 10)` returns 15 characters under the mutant.
  - **Missing case:** `ConflictFileSummary(25 names, 10).Length <= 10` and `ConflictFileSummary(names, -5) == ""` in LandConflictFileSummaryTests.
- **M4b (missing witness).**
  - The suffix reserve matters when the last admitted name lands within 32 characters of the budget. Very_long uses 200-character clipped names (202 per slot), so the 10th name overshoots 2000 either way.
  - **Missing case:** 25 names of 198 characters. Real output keeps 9 names and ends `... and 16 more`. The mutant keeps 10 names (1998 characters), and the final cut eats the suffix.
- **R1 (missing witness).**
  - The existing Merge-role cases use 240-character refs. Their fixed text is about 1100 characters, so `min(2000, 3400 - fixed)` is already 2000 and the sizing is never exercised.
  - **Missing case:** a source ref of about 1500 characters plus 80 names of 4096 characters. Real output keeps the tail sentence; the mutant's list pushes the text past 3400, and the hard cut removes "No rebase resolver was spawned." and the push command.
- **R1b (missing witness).**
  - **Missing case:** a 4000-character TargetFullRef (fixed text over 3400, so the budget is negative) with 80 short names. Real output contains `conflicted in ... and 80 more. `. The mutant gives `conflicted in . `, because the list is clipped to 0 characters.
- **C1 (missing witness).**
  - No test reads the Rebase StageOutcome. `Record` clips it to 1000 characters anyway, so the old raw join cannot throw; it only loses the count.
  - **Missing case:** in C1637_linear_branch_with_many_long_conflicts, assert that the Rebase StageOutcome Detail equals `ConflictFileSummary(names, StageOutcome.DetailMaxLength)`, or at least ends with ` more`.
- **D3 (missing witness: the merge-topology PC).**
  - No test puts a merge commit on the target side. In every existing linear case, master is merge-free.
  - **Missing case:** master has a `--no-ff` merge before the fork and another after it, and the task branch is linear with a conflicting edit. It must still get `rebase_conflict` / NeedsResolution with a resolver.
  - The scratch witness kills D2, D3, D3b and D4 together.
- **D6 (missing witness: needs a fault seam).**
  - Nothing makes `rev-list --min-parents=2` fail.
  - **Missing case:** a delegating ILandingGit in the CreateLand graph that fails exactly that call. Expected: the land refuses with `merge_scan_failed`, and does not fall through to `rebase_conflict` with a resolver.
  - No existing fake delegates to the real git (the fakes in AgentTaskPipelineStatusC1076Tests and the others are hand-rolled), so this needs a small decorator.
- **D7b (missing witness: same seam as D6).**
  - The order (abort, then scan) only matters when the scan throws. With the scan first, a throw from `merge_scan_failed` leaves the land worktree mid-rebase.
  - **Missing case:** the D6 fault plus `RebaseStateDirectoryAsync(op.LandWorktreePath)` should be null after the refusal.
  - Once that exists, D6 and D7b fall together.
- **D10 (missing witness, not equivalent).**
  - A merge-commit branch whose rebase fails with no unmerged paths must stay `rebase_failed`.
  - **Missing case:** a C1637 merge-branch fixture with a failing `pre-rebase` hook in the scratch repository. The hooks are shared by the land worktree. Expected: `rebase_failed`, not `merge_branch_needs_merge_role`.
