## D-1

Where: `SessionMessageQueueService.RecoverDeliveryRunLockedAsync` (`retainOnClear` and the aged path) together with `ClassifyTypedComposerAsync` / `TryReadComposerRegion` for kinds that have no composer box. Fed by the launch retain in `AgentSessionService` and by `DeliverAsync` keeping `Sent` with a null verdict.

Failure: After an ambiguous Input, a Grok session whose native transcript has no UserPrompt is not confirmed by `LateConfirmAttemptedMessagesAsync` or by `GrokRulesRefreshService.Judge` (both require a UserPrompt; a late wire ACK does not set the verdict). `TryReadComposerRegion` is true only for Claude. For Grok, `HeadFragmentIsVisibleWhole` treats any 40-character head that is a substring of the whole normalized screen as `HeadOccupied`. A submitted prompt that is still in scrollback ("Waiting for response") therefore takes Enter-only: `retainOnClear` skips that Enter only when the snapshot is missing or the head is not visible. `EnterOnlyConfirmLockedAsync` writes `\r` before it waits for a transcript. After `InterruptedAttemptAge` (30+20+30 seconds) `retainOnClear` is off. If the head is no longer whole and `DeliveryAttempts` is still under 3, the row reverts to Pending and the ordinary path retypes the body. The rules-closed hold (`IsClosed` is Pending or Failed) applies only when some fresh row has `RulesRefreshKey` null, so the rules prompt itself is not held. `HandleDeliveryFailureAsync` returns immediately for a rules row, leaving it `Sent` with a null verdict, so Enter-only repeats until the attempt cap. A non-rules row is reverted and can be retyped on the next flush.

Why: Unknown delivery is resolved only by a UserPrompt. Screen visibility is treated as "still unsubmitted." For Grok that visibility includes scrollback of a prompt that already submitted. The incident shape (rules text submitted, transcript empty) used to die by SIGKILL, so the retry could not hit the same TUI. The retain now delivers the duplicate to the live process. `GrokRulesReadyOrderingTests` and `AgentSessionLaunchFailureTests` assert `attempts == 1` at the fault instant. They do not advance the clock past `InterruptedAttemptAge` and they do not supply a Grok screen, so the mutant "re-send on uncertain" does not go red for this later flush. CP-6's Enter-only cases are the real "body still in the composer" path.

Fix: While an attempt is Sent, verdict null, and no UserPrompt has matched, do not Enter-only and do not retype from rendered-screen `HeadOccupied` or `Clear` on an agent with no composer box. Hold that attempt until a transcript UserPrompt or an operator acts. Add a test whose Grok snapshot shows the submitted body and "Waiting for response", with zero transcript rows, flushed inside the fresh window and again after `InterruptedAttemptAge`, and assert no second `SendInput` or Enter and that attempts stay 1.
