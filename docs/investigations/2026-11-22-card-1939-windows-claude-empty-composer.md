## What distinguishes the failures

- **First attempt and Enter.** The two retained warnings report the `NoComposerEvidence` refund branch, with zero transcript activity, roughly 60 s before a stranded row is eligible for retry and an eight-minute refund grace. The normal queue path stamps `LastDeliveryStartedAt` before writing the body, checks the rendered screen after the write returns, withholds Enter if evidence is missing and refunds the pre-first-turn attempt without clearing that timestamp. The incident alone cannot distinguish invisible or dropped body input from a snapshot/evidence failure or a guarded Enter refusal. The late `TranscriptMissing` message confirms only that input reached the child at some point. No matching `UserPrompt` proves submission. The Codex-specific complete-pointer consumption gate does not run for Claude. The original delivery log would show whether the normal `submit Enter withheld` warning fired and whether the wire body was inline or a spill pointer. [Attempt stamp](../../server/Application/Services/SessionMessageQueueService.cs#L2952) · [Write and verification](../../server/Application/Services/SessionMessageQueueService.cs#L4394) · [Refund](../../server/Application/Services/SessionMessageQueueService.cs#L5497) · [Codex pointer predicate](../../server/Application/Services/SessionMessageQueueService.CodexPointers.cs#L16)
- **Readiness and subsequent admission.** The failed sessions made it past the initial Running/queue admission boundary. The timed readiness-probe observations were unavailable. CARD-1588’s three hint-row patches (`eb284614b`, `4c4ad5126`, `1f650acc5`) are ancestors of both the reported AppHost and inspected master. `ClaudeScreen.TryReadComposer` still rejects unknown rows below the composer. That classifier can explain a later pre-write `Claude dialog screen unknown` refusal if the contemporaneous frame matches, but the ordinary after-write `ComposerDeliveryEvidence.IsVisible` uses head/tail fragments or a new paste placeholder. It does not call `TryReadComposer`. The historical supervision refusals prove the dialog gate blocked at least one later flush per session. The brief’s zero withhold count does not rule out the same veto on that flush: withhold recording excludes a row with a prior attempt timestamp. Whether each earlier retry was considered, and why no later attempt timestamp persisted, require the retry/snapshot logs. - **Other delivery gates.** The failure rows do not have the CARD-1892 `Sent`/null-verdict/charged-attempt shape. The recorded bind refusals came after the composer-evidence failures, and the transcript search found zero cwd matches, so binding alone cannot explain away the failed submissions. All three launched after their pre-dispatch holds. No retained event records a model hold or profile 404 after dispatch. The concurrency cap and lease explain observed queue delays, without explaining the after-write evidence verdict. A runner restart or transient snapshot lag remains possible. The same-load-window success rules out a continuous failure across all desktop launches but cannot prove equal per-process load or equal runner/Claude versions.
- **Cleanup.** The boot watchdog commits failure separately from process cleanup. Its `CleanupPermittedAsync` requires fresh non-Working evidence and positive runner absence, so listed or unknown inventory withholds cleanup. `RemoveEphemeralAgentAsync` puts a still-live pool delegate in janitor custody. The ordinary pool-release sweep excludes agents already marked idle for the janitor. The janitor protects a Failed live owner and treats zero transcript rows as unproved idle. This combination is consistent with the recorded survivors and is still present on master. There is no historical cleanup decision or runner-inventory line to identify the exact branch on either attempt. Any release proposal must prove the exact generation, current ownership, idle/non-Working state and input custody before conditional release.
