# CARD-2072: BuildSlotClient tests that mix a fake clock with a real-time deadline

Date: 2026-11-25. Task 536d46db (Debug), branch `feat/card-task-536d46db`, base `6f5b24b75`.
Lane: server2 runner mirror, Linux, serial through `scripts/build-slot.ps1`. Test-only.

## Shape

`BuildSlotClient.AcquireAsync` arms its admission deadline as
`new CancellationTokenSource(_wait, _timeProvider)`. A test that injects a fake `clock` or
`delay` and a finite `wait`, but no `timeProvider`, runs that deadline on `TimeProvider.System`
while its busy loop runs on fake time. A loaded host can then fire the deadline before the
broker answers. That shape failed
`CheckpointAdmissionContractTests.C1616_admission_timeout_publishes_readable_nonexecuted_receipt`
in Windows rc run `b8cbfb24` group 112
([why missed](2026-11-25-why-missed-group-112-admission-reason.md)). That site was fixed by
`3d3876410` (`124a379c3` on master).

## Search

A syntactic scan of every `BuildSlotClient` construction under `tests/` found 14 constructions.
Two more sites had the shape at base `6f5b24b75`. The why-missed note's grep missed both,
because they pass `wait`, `clock` and `delay` positionally:

- `BuildSlotClientTests.Client` (line 293) passed `grace, wait, () => now, Delay` with no
  `timeProvider`. `timeout_is_exit_4` passes `wait: 1 ms`, so its deadline was real time
  beside a fake busy loop: whether it timed out depended on 1 ms of real time running out
  before the scripted handler was exhausted.
- `RunSchedulerTests.concurrent_rows_hold_distinct_leases_and_their_lines_say_granted`
  (line 362) passed `wait: 2 s` with a no-op `delay` and no `timeProvider`. Both rows must be
  granted within two real seconds.

The other constructions either inject `timeProvider`
(`CheckpointAdmissionContractTests` lines 427 and 503) or keep the 45-minute default wait.

## Fix

Both sites now inject a `ControlledTimeProvider` that the acquisition delay advances
(`b0f8761a5`). A finite wait now expires only on controlled time. `timeout_is_exit_4` times out
on the first busy retry's advance, not when real time happens to run out.

## Guard

`tests/Antiphon.Tests/Checkpoints/BuildSlotClientClockGuardTests.cs` (Unit) parses every `.cs`
file under `tests/` with Roslyn. It finds explicit and target-typed `BuildSlotClient`
constructions and maps positional arguments to parameter names through the reflected
constructor. It fails when `wait` and either `clock` or `delay` are supplied and `timeProvider`
is not. A `null` or `default` argument counts as not supplied. Vacuity floors require at least
10 constructions, at least 3 with clock/delay, and at least one with `timeProvider`. Pinned
self-tests flag the verbatim pre-fix C1616 construction from `23cdd650` and accept the fixed one
from `3d387641`. They also cover the positional, qualified, default-wait, null-wait, real-time
and target-typed shapes.
